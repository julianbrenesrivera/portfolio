# Strategic account management for Amazon US sellers
**Amazon Strategic Account Services · Customer Success · Portfolio Growth**

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Scope

I managed a portfolio of **20 Amazon US sellers** and progressed from Customer Success Manager to Strategic Account Manager and then Senior Strategic Account Manager.

## Account-management approach

My work combined:

- seller relationships and recurring customer conversations;
- business reviews and growth planning;
- analysis of marketplace performance;
- traffic and conversion signals to inform priorities;
- coordination of next actions across the account.

I also developed a diagnostic tool that helped structure traffic and conversion analysis for other CSMs and account managers.

## Outcome

The portfolio recorded approximately **35% average year-over-year growth in gross merchandise sales (GMS)**.

## What this demonstrates

This case is evidence of strategic account ownership rather than a single campaign or isolated intervention. It combines customer communication, commercial analysis, prioritization, and the ability to operate across multiple seller businesses at the same time.

## Transferable capability

The core operating pattern is applicable beyond marketplaces:

**business signals → account priorities → stakeholder conversation → coordinated action → business review**

[See the portfolio operating model](../artifacts/customer-growth-operating-model.md).

## Evidence boundaries

The growth figure is an approximate portfolio-level outcome. It is not a guarantee, a retention metric, or revenue generated exclusively by my work. It should not be relabeled as ARR, NRR, or company revenue. Individual seller sales and internal account records are not disclosed.
