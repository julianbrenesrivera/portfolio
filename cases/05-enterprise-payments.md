# Enterprise merchant account management
**PayPal Mexico · Large Enterprise Account Executive · Payments**

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Scope

I managed approximately **25 large-enterprise merchants** across retail, ecommerce, and travel.

## Account-management approach

My work included:

- account planning;
- payment adoption;
- cross-sell and upsell opportunities;
- KPI reviews;
- stakeholder coordination;
- monthly enterprise business reviews.

Commercial initiatives included installments (*meses sin intereses*) and Hot Sale activity.

## Translating performance into customer conversations

I used Salesforce, Excel, Tableau, and PowerPoint to support account work and business discussions. The objective was not simply reporting metrics: it was using account performance to structure priorities, commercial conversations, and coordinated follow-up.

## What this demonstrates

- enterprise relationship management;
- payments domain experience;
- commercial account planning;
- recurring executive/business review discipline;
- translating account performance into customer-facing decisions.
