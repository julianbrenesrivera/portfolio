# Enterprise merchant account management
**PayPal Mexico · Large Enterprise Account Executive · Payments**

## Scope
I managed approximately 25 large-enterprise merchants across retail, ecommerce, and travel.

## My contribution
My work included account planning, payment adoption, cross-sell/upsell opportunities, KPI reviews, stakeholder coordination, and monthly enterprise business reviews. Commercial initiatives included installments (meses sin intereses) and Hot Sale activity.

I used Salesforce, Excel, Tableau, and PowerPoint to support account work and business discussions.

## What this demonstrates
Enterprise relationship management, payments knowledge, commercial planning, and the ability to translate account performance into customer conversations.

## Evidence boundaries
This case does not claim software engineering, payment integration implementation, or a quantified conversion increase. Customer-specific payment volumes and contract details are not published. Payment volume must not be relabeled as PayPal revenue.
