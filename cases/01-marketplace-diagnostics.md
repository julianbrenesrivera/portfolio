# Scaling marketplace diagnostics
**Amazon Strategic Account Services · Excel/macros · Enablement**

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Business context

My work with Amazon US sellers required interpreting Seller Central reports and turning traffic and conversion information into useful account priorities.

## My role

Alongside managing seller relationships, I built an Excel/macros tool that organized traffic and conversion data into a four-quadrant diagnostic framework.

## Approach

The goal was to make a recurring account-management task more structured:

1. organize traffic and conversion signals;
2. place the account or opportunity into a consistent diagnostic view;
3. use that view to focus the next account conversation;
4. make the framework usable beyond my own portfolio.

The public portfolio does not reproduce the original thresholds, code, customer data, or internal materials.

## Execution and collaboration

I presented the tool to North American leadership, worked with Product Management on rollout, and served as an official subject-matter expert for training and troubleshooting.

## Outcome

The tool was adopted by **more than 300 CSMs and account managers** across Costa Rica, Chicago, and Seattle.

## What this demonstrates

- translating account data into a repeatable decision framework;
- building analytical tooling for customer-facing teams;
- presenting work to senior stakeholders;
- cross-functional collaboration with Product Management;
- enablement and subject-matter expertise at scale.
