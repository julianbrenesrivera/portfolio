# Scaling marketplace diagnostics
**Amazon Strategic Account Services · Excel/macros · Enablement**

[Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Context
My work with Amazon US sellers included interpreting Seller Central reports and identifying growth opportunities through traffic and conversion analysis.

## My contribution
I built an Excel/macros tool that organized traffic and conversion data into a four-quadrant diagnostic framework. I presented the tool to North American leadership, worked with Product Management on rollout, and served as an official subject-matter expert for training and troubleshooting.

## Outcome
The tool was adopted by more than 300 CSMs and account managers across Costa Rica, Chicago, and Seattle.

## Why this work matters
This case combines customer-facing domain knowledge, analytical tooling, cross-functional collaboration, and enablement. It demonstrates how I translated a recurring account-management task into a resource other account managers could use.

## Evidence boundaries
Adoption is the supported outcome. I do not claim a measured time saving, revenue uplift, or conversion improvement attributable to this tool. Original code, thresholds, customer reports, and internal rollout materials are not included.
