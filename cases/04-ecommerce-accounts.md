# Managing ecommerce account strategy
**AO2 Marketing · Strategic Account Manager · Amazon US**

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Scope

I managed approximately **10 Amazon US client accounts**.

## Account-management responsibilities

My work connected commercial goals with day-to-day marketplace decisions across:

- account strategy and forecasting;
- pricing and promotions;
- inventory and catalog;
- Account Health;
- reporting and client meetings.

My analysis included GMS, traffic, conversion, inventory performance, and TACOS.

## Cross-functional model

Paid advertising was coordinated with a PPC specialist. My role was account strategy and coordination; the specialist handled direct PPC execution.

That distinction matters: the account manager owned the broader customer context and priorities while specialist execution remained with the relevant expert.

## Outcome

The portfolio performed approximately **10% above targets**.

## What this demonstrates

- multi-account portfolio management;
- commercial and operational ecommerce judgment;
- turning marketplace metrics into customer priorities;
- coordinating specialist work without overstating hands-on ownership;
- communicating performance in recurring client conversations.

## Evidence boundaries

“Above targets” does not mean 10% year-over-year growth or a 10-percentage-point conversion increase. No independently attributable advertising ROAS or hands-on PPC ownership is claimed.
