## The operating loop

Across strategic account management, ecommerce, payments, and lifecycle work, a recurring pattern in my experience is:

### 1. Diagnose
Start with business and customer signals.

Examples from my experience include traffic, conversion, GMS, inventory performance, TACOS, payment/account KPIs, and lifecycle progression.

### 2. Prioritize
Analysis is only useful if it changes what happens next.

The objective is to distinguish the most relevant growth opportunity, customer need, operational blocker, or account risk from lower-priority noise.

### 3. Align
Turn the analysis into a conversation that customers and internal stakeholders can act on.

This can happen through business reviews, account meetings, enablement, reporting, or cross-functional coordination.

### 4. Execute
Coordinate the agreed work.

Depending on the context, execution may involve the account owner, the customer, Product Management, legal counsel, a PPC specialist, or other partners.

### 5. Review
Return to the business signals and assess what changed.

Business reviews, recurring reporting, and experimentation create the feedback loop for the next decision.

## Why this matters

The industries and tools can change. The transferable capability is the operating pattern:

**understand the signal → choose the priority → align people → execute → learn**

That is the connective tissue across my work in strategic accounts, customer growth, ecommerce, payments, and SaaS.
